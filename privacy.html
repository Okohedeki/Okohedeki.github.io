Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Policy scope

This policy covers this public website and the internal keyword research tool described on it.

Return to overview →

1. Overview

Okohedeki is developing an internal, read-only keyword research tool for Google Ads accounts that the operator owns or is authorized to manage. The tool is not offered to the public and does not provide account access to third parties.

2. Information collected by this website

This website does not include account registration, contact forms, advertising pixels, or analytics scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process standard technical information in accordance with the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Google Ads information processed by the tool

When connected to Google Ads, the internal tool may process account identifiers, keyword research inputs, keyword ideas, historical search metrics, competition indicators, bid-range estimates, and forecast metrics. Processing is limited to research and planning for authorized accounts.

4. Authorization

Google OAuth will be used to authorize access to eligible Google Ads accounts. The tool will not request or collect Google account passwords. Access may be revoked through the applicable Google account settings.

5. Use and sharing

Google Ads information will be used only for internal keyword research, planning, tool operation, and troubleshooting. It will not be sold, published, used to provide a public data service, or shared with external users.

6. Retention and protection

Credentials and retrieved information will be retained only for as long as needed to operate and maintain the internal tool. Reasonable technical and organizational measures will be used to restrict access to the operator and protect stored credentials and account data.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the tool's implementation changes. Any material expansion beyond the read-only, internal-use scope described here will be documented before that change is placed into production.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to Okohedeki through the operator's public GitHub profile.
