1. Purpose

This website documents an internal keyword research tool operated by Okohedeki. The tool is intended to retrieve keyword ideas, historical search metrics, competition indicators, bid ranges, and forecasts for Google Ads accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage.

2. Authorized use

Use is limited to the operator and authorized advertising accounts. The tool is not made available for use by clients, customers, or other external parties.

3. Read-only limitation

The planned tool does not create, edit, or delete campaigns, ads, ad groups, budgets, targeting settings, or account keywords. Research outputs are informational and do not execute advertising decisions.

4. No indirect API access

The tool does not offer a secondary API, share the Google Ads API developer token, or allow third parties to make requests through the operator's credentials.

5. Google services

Use of Google Ads services remains subject to Google's applicable terms, policies, account permissions, and service availability. Google Ads is a trademark of Google LLC. This tool is not endorsed by or affiliated with Google.

6. Availability and changes

The tool is in development and may change or be unavailable. If its API use materially changes, the public description and applicable policy documentation will be updated before the expanded use is placed into production.
