Research outputs

A narrow tool with a defined purpose.

The tool supports keyword planning and account research. It does not function as a public keyword database, campaign manager, or access layer for other people.

  1. 01

    Keyword ideas

    Generate related keyword suggestions from seed terms or eligible account context.

    Discovery
  2. 02

    Historical metrics

    Review available search-volume trends and historical demand indicators.

    Demand history
  3. 03

    Competition and bid ranges

    Compare competition indicators and available top-of-page bid estimates.

    Market context
  4. 04

    Forecasts

    Evaluate forecast metrics for prospective keyword plans before any manual campaign decision.

    Planning

Operational boundary

Research in. Decisions stay with the account owner.

+ The tool will

  • Authenticate access to eligible accounts
  • Submit read-only keyword-planning requests
  • Display research outputs for internal analysis
  • Keep the operator responsible for advertising decisions

− The tool will not

  • Create or modify campaigns, ads, budgets, or keywords
  • Offer account access to clients or outside users
  • Resell, publish, or expose Google Ads account data
  • Provide another API backed by the developer token

Access and data

Designed for controlled internal use.

Only Google Ads accounts that the operator owns or is authorized to manage are within scope.

AUTH

Authorized accounts only

Google OAuth will be used to authorize access. The tool will not collect Google account passwords.

USE

Purpose-limited processing

Retrieved information will be used only for internal keyword research, planning, and troubleshooting.

SHARE

No external users

The tool will not expose the operator's Google Ads access, developer token, or account data to third parties.

Detailed handling practices and site terms are published separately.
